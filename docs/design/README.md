# Design direction

Mock A · Paper, chosen 2026-10-16 from three clickable mocks:
https://claude.ai/artifact/5L2N2GLe9zD74En3PBBdWU (switch to "A · Paper" at the top).

- **Layout:** an always-visible sidebar tree on desktop (a drawer on phones), breadcrumbs,
  back/forward, and a single reading column of about 46 rem.
- **Type:** IBM Plex Serif for headings and IBM Plex Sans for text and UI.
- **Colour:** cool off-white paper with ink-blue accents, plus a dark theme. Tokens are in the mock's `:root`.
- **Cards:** hover cards float over the text on desktop; on touch they open as a bottom sheet.
- **Inline actions:** proposals show dashed accent boxes with ✓ / ✗, and comments show a
  left highlight bar with a thread popover.

The mocks use real note content for realism, but they are not a content source.
